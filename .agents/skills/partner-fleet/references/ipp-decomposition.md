# Ideal Partner Profile (IPP) Decomposition Guide

This guide teaches the agent how to take any ecosystem strategy or partnership mandate and break it into high-precision technical search criteria for discovering implementation partners.

---

## The 3-Signal Framework for Implementation Partners

When an operator says:
> *"I need systems integrators who can deploy our vector database for enterprise clients."*
> *"We want boutique agencies that migrate companies from legacy MySQL to ClickHouse."*
> *"Find consultancies with deep Snowflake and dbt practices that work with financial services."*

Never search for broad terms like "consulting firms" or "tech agencies". Deconstruct the requirement into **Signals**:

### 1. Ecosystem / Capability Signals (What tech do they specialize in?)
Identify the core platform and adjacent dependencies the partner must already be deploying:
- Primary Platform: `Snowflake`, `Databricks`, `ClickHouse`, `Supabase`, `Kafka`, `Kubernetes`, `Datadog`
- Complementary Stack: `dbt`, `Terraform`, `Airflow`, `Python`, `AWS EKS`, `PostgreSQL`
- Certifications / Alliances: `AWS Premier Tier`, `Snowflake Elite Partner`, `Datadog Gold Partner`

### 2. Service Model & Delivery Proof Signals (How do they deliver value?)
Ensure the entity is an actual professional services firm that takes delivery ownership, not a software company or recruiting agency:
- **Systems Integration & Turnkey Deployment**: *"full-lifecycle implementation"*, *"systems integration"*, *"client deployment"*, *"architecture advisory"*
- **Migration & Modernization**: *"legacy migration services"*, *"cloud modernization practice"*, *"zero-downtime migration for clients"*
- **Client Case Studies**: *"Case Study: How we helped [Client] deploy..."*, *"delivered a multi-region cluster for an enterprise customer"*
- **Managed Services**: *"24/7 managed cloud operations"*, *"managed platform engineering for clients"*

### 3. Staffing / Hiring Urgency Signals (Are they actively delivering?)
Look for job titles that prove active client billing and expanding delivery capacity:
- `Solutions Architect` / `Enterprise Solutions Architect`
- `Implementation Consultant` / `Senior Technical Consultant`
- `Practice Lead` / `Practice Director`
- `Technical Delivery Manager` / `Engagement Lead`
- `Systems Integration Engineer`

## Translation Examples

### Example A: "We need boutique SIs to implement Kafka and streaming pipelines for clients"
- **Stack Keywords**: `Apache Kafka`, `Kafka Streams`, `Flink`, `AWS MSK`, `Kubernetes`
- **Service Keywords**: *"streaming practice"*, *"client implementation"*, *"event-driven migration"*, *"consulting services"*
- **Hiring Roles**: `Solutions Architect`, `Streaming Consultant`, `Data Platform Architect`
- **ATS Search Query**:
  `site:jobs.ashbyhq.com OR site:boards.greenhouse.io "Apache Kafka" ("client delivery" OR "consulting" OR "implementation") "Architect"`

### Example B: "We want agencies that deploy modern AI / LLM workflows for mid-market clients"
- **Stack Keywords**: `LangChain`, `LlamaIndex`, `OpenAI`, `vLLM`, `Pinecone`, `Qdrant`
- **Service Keywords**: *"AI consulting"*, *"generative AI implementation"*, *"client AI solutions"*, *"custom LLM integration"*
- **Hiring Roles**: `AI Solutions Architect`, `Lead AI Consultant`, `Applied AI Engineer`
- **ATS Search Query**:
  `site:jobs.ashbyhq.com ("generative AI" OR "LLM") ("consulting" OR "agency" OR "client engagement") "Architect"`

### Example C: "We need data consultancies that migrate clients from Redshift to Snowflake"
- **Stack Keywords**: `Snowflake`, `Redshift`, `dbt`, `Airflow`, `AWS`
- **Service Keywords**: *"cloud data migration"*, *"Snowflake Premier Partner"*, *"data modernization services"*, *"client delivery"*
- **Hiring Roles**: `Snowflake Solutions Architect`, `Senior Data Consultant`, `Analytics Delivery Lead`
- **ATS Search Query**:
  `site:boards.greenhouse.io OR site:jobs.lever.co "Snowflake" "migration" ("consulting" OR "clients") "Architect"`
